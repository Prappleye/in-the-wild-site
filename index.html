Safety & privacy

You decide who you connect with.

  • Your first and last names are kept private from other users.
  • Connections happen after meeting in person at an event.
  • Report and block anyone, any time.

FAQ

Where can I get the app?

In the Wild is an iOS app currently in beta on TestFlight. It’s coming soon to the App Store. Email us to request a beta invite.

How do I find events near me?

Discover shows events within 25 miles by default, based on your location or a ZIP code. You can change the distance and filter by date, time and interests.

Why do I need a passphrase?

The host shares it at the event, so checking in confirms you were actually there.

Who can see my name?

Your first and last names are kept private. Others see your username and profile.

What if a host cancels?

If you marked the event interested, you’ll get a cancellation message from the host.
