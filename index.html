Show Up In the Wild

Show Up In the Wild (appearing on your home screen as In the Wild) is an iOS app that helps people attend real-life events, verify they actually showed up, and meet the other attendees. The app is currently in TestFlight beta and is coming soon to the App Store.

Attend events

Discover and join in-person events happening near you.

Verify you showed up

Confirm your attendance at the event so everyone knows who is really there.

Meet other attendees

Connect with the people who showed up and keep the conversation going.

About

In the Wild, LLC is a Utah limited liability company based in Saratoga Springs, Utah. We believe the best moments happen in person. We build simple, respectful tools that help people show up, meet new people, and spend less time behind a screen.
